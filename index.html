Smoky Chickpea Skillet

Tomatoes, chickpeas, paprika, and a little lemon. Cozy without the fuss.

Ingredients

1 can chickpeas, 1 can tomatoes, 1 onion, 2 cloves garlic, 1 tsp smoked paprika, olive oil, lemon.

Method

Soften diced onion in olive oil. Add garlic and paprika, then tomatoes and drained chickpeas. Simmer 15 minutes. Finish with lemon.
